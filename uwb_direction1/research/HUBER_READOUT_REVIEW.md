# Bounded residual influence: fixed-scale Huber readout

Question: do unusually large source-training onehot regression residuals pull the decision surface away from source-user transferable predictions? This is distinct from penalizing within-class user means, optimizing worst user loss or deleting whole users. None of those identifies clip/output residual influence. It is a hypothesis, not evidence that the dataset has wrong labels. No test errors are used to choose clips or thresholds.

Sources read on2026-10-07: [SciPy Huber definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.special.huber.html), [sklearn1.8 HuberRegressor documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.HuberRegressor.html), and sklearn1.8 `_huber.py` loss/gradient, Git blob c5fee4a0b1f5049a38c35a82aa615427d20c4368. The loss is quadratic inside delta and linear outside; its residual derivative clips at delta. sklearn jointly optimizes scale and defaults epsilon1.35. Our delta stays fixed from source ridge MAD, hence is not that estimator. The1.35 choice is a documented default, not a theorem for onehot residuals. The normal MAD factor is a scale convention, not an assertion that class residuals are Gaussian. Huber/Ronchetti Robust Statistics and Owen2006 are references in the docs; original full texts were not successfully accessed. Huber1964, Annals of Mathematical Statistics35(1):73–101, DOI10.1214/aoms/1177703732 concerns location estimation, not UWB cross-user guarantees. No originality or radar-specific validity is claimed from these references.

We independently implement the fixed-scale convex objective. Training-only original standardization and equal-user/equal-present-class weights are retained. All12 outputs get independent intercepts and coefficients; hard classification takes the largest score. The primary is fully optimized Huber. One-step IRLS separates initial residual downweighting from iterative adaptation. Permuting final influence weights within training user/class cells preserves their distribution/mass in each cell while destroying clip-weight association; it still conditions on fitted primary weights, so is a mechanistic null, not independently selected competitor. Original full-source ridge is the strong control. No weights are renormalized after clipping/permutation, to preserve the defined loss penalty scale.

Feature leverage is not controlled by clipping residual derivatives. Suppression of difficult legitimate samples and rare positives is a plausible failure mode. The source encoder has already fit all those clips. Reused user diagnostics remain exploratory; no claim of untouched confirmation.
