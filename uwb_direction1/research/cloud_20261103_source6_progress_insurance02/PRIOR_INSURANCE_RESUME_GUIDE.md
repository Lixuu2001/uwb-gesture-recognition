Four synthetic integrity/race tests pass: complete uncommitted suffix plus exact raw partial cut preservation, committed-prefix tampering, wrong atomic-slot hash and a progress-publication race. A partial trailing append, if any, remains forensic data in the raw cut and is never silently fed to the strict replay parser. Both actual cuts had zero partial bytes.
