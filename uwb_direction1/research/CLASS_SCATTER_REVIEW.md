# Class-conditional scatter feasibility screen

Hypothesis: source classes may occupy different anisotropic clouds in the already frozen128dim gesture representation; a single linear ridge decision boundary may not exploit this. Test known OAS-QDA, not a newly invented classifier. Its positive result would establish a stronger baseline / evidence for a future mechanism, not an original contribution.

QDA score is -.5logdet(Sigma_k)-.5(x-mu_k)^T inv(Sigma_k)(x-mu_k)+logprior. Shared covariance yields LDA; diagonal covariance removes feature correlations. OAS shrinks toward tr(S)/p I and estimates its coefficient from source samples only. sklearn omits the original2/p term: this approximation is recorded and independently reproduced for audit. Gaussian/iid covariance risk claims do not establish classification improvement, especially with repeated correlated clips and cross-user shift. The experiment cannot infer a physical radar covariance from learned hidden coordinates.
