# Raw temporal reversal with semantic relabeling: feasibility, not novelty

Price and Damen, *Retro-Actions: Learning Close by Time-Reversing Open Videos*, ICCV Workshops2019, [arXiv1909.09422v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.09422). Read HTML abstract, sections1–4 and5.3–6, including class-homogeneous label transforms, out-of-vocabulary counterparts, augmentation and reversal-artifact caveats; skimmed intervening zero-shot results, not all references/appendix or visual figures. CVF PDF fetch returned403; do not claim the publisher PDF was read. Their augmentation changes labels when required and excludes non-applicable transforms. Time reversal alone does not universally improve their validation results. Their automatic mapping discovery and end-to-end video training are not implemented here. This establishes direct prior art, not a claim of originality for relabeled augmentation.

Own radar derivation: for analytic frames z_t and increment d_t=z_t*conj(z_(t-1)), reversing90raw frames gives reversed increments conj(d_(89-t)) under a consistent index convention. Their weighted signed angles change sign and order. Historical duplication of the first increment becomes duplication of the other boundary increment; this also changes per-clip mean/std. Therefore merely flipping cached normalized phase inputs is generally wrong. Production recomputes from raw RF; independent verification computes original SciPy Hilbert frames, derives conjugate/reversed increments and repeats the new boundary. No true I/Q or physical velocity claim. Raw time reversal twice is identity, but that algebra alone cannot certify natural gesture execution.
