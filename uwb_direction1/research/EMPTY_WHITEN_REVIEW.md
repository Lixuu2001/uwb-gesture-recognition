# Source-empty spectral prewhitening, a conditional mechanism screen

Luo Zhongtao, He Zishu, Lu Kun and Chen Xuyuan, *Optimal receive filter design under similarity constraint in coloured noise*, IET Radar Sonar & Navigation9(7):888–899,2015, DOI[10.1049/iet-rsn.2014.0421](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1049/iet-rsn.2014.0421). Read publisher abstract, introduction and section2 prose; equations displayed as images were not inspected, later derivation/simulations not fully read. Their model uses known/estimated positive-definite environmental covariance and discusses sidelobe/range-resolution risk of unconstrained whitening. It motivates checking filtering distortion, not a UWB accuracy claim or replication of their receive-filter optimization. No transmitter template/matched detector is implemented here. Also read [SciPy periodogram API and scaling note](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.periodogram.html): a real one-sided PSD doubles interior frequencies; our positive-frequency storage intentionally keeps the undoubled two-sided ordinates for applying a symmetric filter.

Independent derivation: under a circular stationary covariance approximation diagonalized by the DFT, a spectrum P transformed by real symmetric gains H has spectrum H²P. H=(P+alpha*mean(P))^(-1/2) regularizes inverse spectral coloring. Overall unit white-noise gain normalization multiplies all entries by a common constant. This is established prewhitening, not a new mathematical contribution. Alpha=.1 is an explicitly fixed engineering choice, not from the paper and not tuned to test outcomes. Learning P from source empty clips is a hypothesis about nuisance transfer, not proof that empty-class data are noise only. It can remove informative RF pulse structure and amplify weak bands.

The previous range-frequency study tested predefined band complements, and energy-area study used within-radar energy path descriptors. Here a per-source-fold empty class learns frequency color, with same-size raw-block and shuffled-gain controls. Whitening operates only on an appended567-dimensional block-energy representation; frozen CNN features and original ridge baseline remain exactly replayable. No new encoder or support labels for the held user are required. This differentiates the test mechanically but does not establish novelty. Original docs mention low-rank user-factor calibration; that is not raw empty-echo PSD filtering. Keyword search is not an exhaustive prior-art search.

The control permutation fixes DC/Nyquist, permutes interior positive bins independently by radar, and preserves the full-spectrum gain histogram and white-noise power. It breaks learned frequency alignment. We compare the primary against both unfiltered descriptors and the original strong ridge to avoid crediting generic feature enlargement. No secondary control is promoted after primary failure. No claim of Doppler extraction: FFT is along189fast-time range bins, not90slow-time frames. The full512-point finite convolution and crop may distort boundaries. All templates use only training user/class11 IDs; query labels do not influence filtering, fitting, lambda selection or normalization. Reused development users remain exploratory, and any successful screen needs separately frozen eightfold replication and stronger novelty evidence.
