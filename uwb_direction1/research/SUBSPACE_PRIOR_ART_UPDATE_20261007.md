# Supplemental prior-art check during the frozen classifier run

This check changes no protocol, model, threshold, seed or selection rule.

[Ehrnsperger, Noll, Punzet, Siart and Eibert (2021), Dynamic Eigenimage Based Background and Clutter Suppression for Ultra Short-Range Radar](https://ars.copernicus.org/articles/19/71/2021/), DOI10.5194/ars-19-71-2021. Read HTML abstract and sections1–4, including displayed equations and figure captions; figure images and complete numeric table not separately inspected. Their24GHz multistatic FMCW dPCA adapts reconstruction eigenimages and interframe mean removal to avoid suppressing slow/stationary targets. This is direct prior art against claiming adaptive clutter-subspace selection itself as new. Our fixed training-empty range projection and source energy gate differ, but that distinction alone is not novelty. Their detection/SCR setting is not our gesture classification protocol. Reference list identifies Sabushimike et al.2016 as Sensors16:1409, DOI10.3390/s16091409; correct publisher URL was attempted here but unavailable, so that full paper remains unreviewed.

[Xue, Yang and Zhu (2026), Neuromorphic-inspired multi-view global-local fusion for IR-UWB radar dynamic gesture recognition](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2026.1864448/full), published19June2026. Read metadata, abstract, section3.3–3.4 prose,4.1–4.3 tables/prose, conclusion/data statement; equation images and full implementation not reviewed. They report98.29% on UWB-Gestures using a6-training/2-testing-subject split,9,600 nominal samples, MTI TR maps resized64×128,100epochs. This differs from our retained9,300 samples and eightfold train/validation/held evaluation. The reviewed text does not specify split subject IDs or a separate validation/checkpoint rule. Test-accuracy curves are shown; that alone does not establish test-based selection. No GitHub implementation link was found in the page. Thus this is a relevant architecture baseline to investigate, not a directly comparable score or evidence of misconduct. Global/local fusion and gated bilinear combination have dataset-specific prior art; avoid presenting those combinations as new.

Research implication: classification efficacy and originality remain separate requirements. Neither a positive energy proxy nor beating an unmatched published number can establish the user's goal. Any later architecture comparison needs a faithful, executable same-split baseline and training-only decisions; do not alter the current experiment after seeing partial results.
