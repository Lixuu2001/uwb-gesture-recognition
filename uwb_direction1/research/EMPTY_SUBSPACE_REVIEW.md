# Training-empty subspace selectivity audit

[Liu etal2018, Eigensubspace method for space-time adaptive processing in the presence of non-i.i.d. clutter and array errors](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1049/iet-rsn.2017.0482), IET Radar Sonar Navigation12(7):757–765. Read publisher abstract/introduction, section2.3–2.4 prose, and relevant6.2.2 target-contamination discussion. Image equations and full proofs/simulations were not reviewed. Their complementary-subspace method relies on clutter rank and steering structure; the article describes suppression of an injected target when secondary data contain a related return. Our real-RF range covariance is not their airborne angle-Doppler/STAP model or rank estimator. This supports testing target/background separability rather than assuming all dominant empty-echo directions are nuisance.

[scikit-learn1.8 decomposition documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/decomposition.html#pca), exact PCA subsection reviewed. It describes orthogonal variance directions and explicitly centers features. We deliberately analyze uncentered second moments of already ClutterRemoved RF, so do not call this an exact sklearn PCA implementation. Independent algebra: with orthonormal U, P=UU^T is idempotent and ||X(I-P)||F²=||X||F²-||XU||F². Source-only scaling makes each nonzero clip/view have equal total power before fitting, not equal absolute physical scattering power.

The 2016 IR-UWB low-rank recovery article PMC5038687 was located but full-text reads hit a browser challenge/MDPI429; it is not treated as a reviewed RPCA basis. Original docs/RESULTS.md records protocol-dependent Raw versus ClutterRemoved performance; available cache contains ClutterRemoved X, and this experiment does not restore a missing Raw tensor. Original project also already discusses same-rank random subspace controls for teacher-feature distillation. Here the representation and diagnostic differ, but eigenprojections and random controls are established and do not establish novelty.

Why this diagnostic now: the failed empty-spectrum whitening screen did not establish that empty-class energy is removable nuisance. Its Fourier-diagonal filter differs from a learned full189dim range subspace. We therefore test the premise on source users before adding another classifier. Inner checks fit five source users' empty data and evaluate another source user; all six belong to outerfold1 training roles. No user1/2 RF or labels enter fitting or metrics. Sampling, energy coverage, rank capacity and selectivity thresholds are frozen before real computation.

The proxy deliberately asks for substantial empty attenuation while retaining activity energy and outperforming a rank-matched random projector. It cannot distinguish true target echo from shared pulse response/background. Failure only withholds evidence for this particular removal strategy; it does not show that every removed joule is useful gesture information, prove noise separation impossible, or establish classification degradation. No accuracy is measured and no supervised recognition model is trained. Success would merely justify a separately frozen efficacy study, with the same strong baselines and eightfold protocol. This is a mechanism audit with direct prior art, not an innovation claim.
