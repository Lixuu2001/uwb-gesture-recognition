# Source-only gated eigencancellation: prospective classifier screen

The preregistered EMPTY_SUBSPACE mechanism diagnostic passed on left/right and failed on top. This justifies checking classification; it does not establish efficacy. Crucially, the classifier does not hard-code this global view decision. Within each fold, only its four training users run the nested energy-selectivity gate. Validation chooses only the original three ridge strengths. Held users are scored after predictions are saved.

Literature basis and read scope are in [EMPTY_SUBSPACE_REVIEW.md](EMPTY_SUBSPACE_REVIEW.md). Liu et al. (2018), [Eigensubspace method for STAP](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1049/iet-rsn.2017.0482), provides prior art for removing a learned clutter subspace and warns about target contamination. This is not a replication of airborne STAP, its rank estimator, or its nonhomogeneity detector. Uncentered range moments differ from centered PCA. No new paper efficacy claim follows from an energy proxy.

The test isolates a concrete question: can training-only activity-preservation checks prevent harmful radar filtering and improve the same128-dimensional frozen representation? Controls are untouched strong ridge, filtering all radars, and rank-matched random filtering on the same chosen views. Primary must improve ridge by0.5pp and each control by0.25pp, win at least4/6 and lose no fold by more than1pp. Engineering criteria are prospective, not significance levels. No promotion of a winning control or post-result threshold/rank/seed changes. Full eightfold work requires a separate preregistration after passing.

Implementation filters real RF before Hilbert phase recomputation. Every arm retains the same encoder/readout capacity. Source-only gate uses3/4 inner-user passes, preserving the earlier two-thirds criterion. No assumptions that empty returns are pure noise or retained energy is gesture signal. The frozen CNN may not tolerate observation changes; a negative result closes only this specified intervention.
