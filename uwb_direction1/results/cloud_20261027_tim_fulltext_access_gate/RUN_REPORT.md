The exact distinction between the TIM paper’s “views” and this corpus’s three physically separated monostatic X4 radars remains unresolved at the method level. The held-view protocol is not itself an original signal mechanism. Continue only with another materially distinct, literature-cleared mechanism directly observable in the existing clutter-removed maps, or keep this candidate closed until a lawful full text is available.

This outcome is an access limitation, not evidence that a candidate method is scientifically ineffective. The overall contribution goal remains unresolved.
